# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Breakout Workspace and its users safe.

## Supported versions

Only the live version of the app is supported.
It is built from the `main` branch, and fixes are deployed there.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security issues privately, not in a public issue.

- **GitHub:** use [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/VishnuDevi123/breakout-workspace/security/advisories/new) on this repository.
- **Website:** contact details are on the [support page](https://breakout-workspace.vercel.app/support).

Please include:

- what you found and where (the Zoom App, the backend API or the website);
- the steps to reproduce it;
- what an attacker could do with it.

## What happens next

- You get a reply within 2 business days confirming the report was received.
- You get an update within 7 days, with an assessment and a plan.
- Critical and high severity issues are fixed and deployed as soon as possible.
- You are credited when the fix is released, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Disclosure

Your report stays private until a fix is released.
Please keep it private too, until the fix is out or 90 days have passed, whichever comes first.

## Scope

In scope:

- the Breakout Workspace Zoom App;
- the backend API at `breakout-workspace.onrender.com`;
- the website at `breakout-workspace.vercel.app`.

Out of scope:

- Zoom's own platform and clients; please report those to [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/en/trust/vulnerability-disclosure/);
- denial of service tests, spam and social engineering.

## Testing rules

Use only your own Zoom accounts and meetings.
Do not access, change or delete other people's data.
Stop and report as soon as you find an issue.
Research done in good faith within these rules is welcome, and will not lead to legal action.
